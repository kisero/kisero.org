Boat alarm

Connecting to the Pi…

Turning on gives you 30 seconds to leave. Movement then sounds the buzzer for 10 seconds. Turning off stops it once the Pi receives the change.

If the Pi is offline, changes wait until it reconnects. Its last applied setting continues locally. Motion is recorded whether the alarm is on or off.

Keep this private link to yourself: anyone with it can control the alarm.
